# Cloud Credits

Roo Code Cloud uses a flexible credit system to power on-demand resources. **Cloud Credits** are the unified currency used to pay for:
- **Model Inference**: Usage of premium models (outside of free promotional tiers).
- **Agent Execution**: Runtime hours for autonomous cloud agents.
- **Storage**: Long-term retention of task history and artifacts.

<img src="/img/credits/overview.png" alt="Cloud Credits Dashboard" width="800" />

## How Credits Work

1. **Purchase**: You buy credits in packs (e.g., $10 for 1000 credits).
2. **Consume**: As you use cloud resources, credits are deducted from your balance in real-time.
3. **Refill**: You can manually top up or enable auto-refill to never run out.

### Consumption Rates

*Note: Rates are subject to change. Check the [Dashboard](https://app.roocode.com/billing) for live pricing.*

| Resource | Cost (Approx) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Premium Models** | Market rate per 1M tokens (varies by model) |
| **Cloud Agents** | ~10 credits / hour of active execution |
| **Storage** | Free for first 1GB, then 1 credit / GB / month |

:::tip Promotional Period
Currently, many models and agent types are available for **FREE** during our promotional period. You will see a credit cost of `0` in your logs.
:::

## Managing Your Balance

### Auto Top-Up (Recommended)
Ensure your agents never stop mid-task by enabling Auto Top-Up.

1. Go to **Billing & Credits** in your [Dashboard](https://app.roocode.com/billing).
2. Toggle **Auto Top-Up** to ON.
3. Set your **Threshold** (e.g., "When balance falls below 500 credits").
4. Set your **Refill Amount** (e.g., "Add 2000 credits").

### Low Balance Alerts
You will receive email notifications when your balance drops below:
- 20% of your average daily usage
- A fixed threshold you define

## Advantages of Credits

- **Pay for Value**: You only pay when agents are actually working for you.
- **No Wasted Capacity**: Unlike fixed subscriptions with seat limits, credits scale perfectly with your workload.
- **Unified Billing**: One bill for all models (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) and all agent types.
- **Team Sharing**: Credits are pooled at the Team level, so anyone in your organization can use them.

## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
Purchased credits never expire. Promotional or "gift" credits may have an expiration date specified at the time of issuance.

**Can I transfer credits?**
Credits can be shared within a Team organization but cannot be transferred between different personal accounts.

**What happens if I run out of credits mid-task?**
The task will pause and wait for a top-up. We provide a grace buffer to allow current operations to finish cleanly, but subsequent actions will be blocked until the balance is positive.
